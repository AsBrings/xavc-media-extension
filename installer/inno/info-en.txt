XAVC S / S-I decoder

After installation, the Windows Media Player app, Explorer thumbnails and other Media Foundation programs can play Sony XAVC S and XAVC S-I videos (H.264 High 4:2:2 10-bit, .MP4).

• Only H.264 streams the built-in Windows decoder cannot handle (4:2:2, 10-bit, 4:4:4) are taken over; all other MP4 files keep using the Windows decoder.
• Decoding runs on the CPU (multi-threaded); NVIDIA GPUs that can decode H.264 4:2:2 (RTX 50 series) are used automatically.
• When the computer cannot decode fast enough (e.g. 4K 60p on a thin laptop), frames are skipped to keep video and audio in sync.
• Restart Media Player if it is running.
• Existing thumbnails are not refreshed automatically; you can clear the thumbnail cache on the last page of this wizard.
• Uninstall: Settings > Apps > Installed apps > XAVC S / S-I Decoder.

Requires 64-bit (x64) Windows 10 or 11.
